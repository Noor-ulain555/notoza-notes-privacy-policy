Privacy Policy for Notoza

App package: com.technoorstudio.notoza
Effective date: 17 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Notoza (the “App”), developed by Techno Or Studio (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”), handles information when you use it. Notoza is a note-taking application. We designed it to keep your notes on your own device. This policy describes what stays on your device, what limited information is processed by the third-party services the App uses, and the choices available to you.

In short: Your notes are stored locally on your device and are not sent to us or to any server we control. We do not have accounts, and we cannot read your notes. The App shows ads (Google AdMob), measures anonymous usage (Google Firebase Analytics), and reports crashes (Google Firebase Crashlytics); those Google services process limited technical and usage data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The following data is created and stored locally on your device only (in the App's private database and settings storage). It is not transmitted to the developer:

  • Note content — the title and body text of each note (including rich-text formatting), an optional category label, a color label, pin/favorite status, and creation/modification timestamps.
  • Trash data — notes you delete are soft-deleted and kept in a local Trash until removed, along with the time they were trashed.
  • App preferences — your chosen theme (light/dark/system), your note sort order, and a first-launch flag.

Because this data lives only on your device, deleting the App (or clearing its storage) removes it. We have no ability to access, recover, or restore it.

2. Device backup (Android Auto Backup)

The App participates in Android's standard Auto Backup feature. If it is enabled on your device, Android may automatically back up the App's local data (your notes and preferences) to your own Google Drive account. This backup is managed by Android and Google, is tied to your Google Account, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can control or disable this in your device's system backup settings. The developer does not have access to these backups.

3. Information processed by third-party services

To show ads and understand general usage, the App integrates the following Google services. These services may collect information automatically as described in their own policies:

3.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The App displays advertisements using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), including native, interstitial, and app-open ad formats. To serve, personalize, cap, and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • Device and technical information (e.g., device model, operating system version, coarse language/region, general network information);
  • Ad interaction data (e.g., ad impressions and clicks).

The App requests the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission for this purpose. AdMob's handling of this data is described in Google's Advertising and Privacy policies. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy / Google → Ads).

3.2 Google Firebase Analytics (usage measurement)

The App uses Firebase Analytics to understand how features are used, in aggregate. It logs anonymous, app-level events such as when a note is created, updated, deleted, restored, or pinned; when the theme or sort order is changed; when the app is shared; and when Settings, Trash, or this privacy policy is opened. A search event is logged when you use in-app search, but the text you type is not included — only the anonymous fact that a search occurred is recorded, so your note and search content never leaves your device. Firebase Analytics also collects standard automatic events (screen views, session start, first open), an app-instance identifier, and general device/technical information.

These events are used only to measure and improve the App. Firebase Analytics data is handled under Google's Firebase Privacy and Security terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

3.3 Google Firebase Crashlytics (crash reporting)

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics to detect and diagnose crashes and stability problems. When the App crashes or encounters a non-fatal error, Crashlytics automatically collects a diagnostic crash report, which may include:

  • The stack trace and technical details of the crash or error;
  • Device state and technical information (e.g., device model, operating system version, App version, available memory/storage, and whether the App was in the foreground);
  • A randomly generated installation identifier used to group reports from the same installation.

Crashlytics does not intentionally collect the contents of your notes or personally identifying information. Crash data is used solely to fix bugs and improve reliability, and is handled under Google's Firebase Privacy and Security terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, location, photos, or files.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We (the developer) do not receive or store the contents of your notes on any server.

6. Data sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own purposes. The limited technical and usage data described in Section 3 is processed by Google (AdMob and Firebase) acting as our service provider, for the purposes of advertising and analytics only.

7. Data retention

Your notes and preferences remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Any backup you have enabled is retained by Google according to your account settings. Data processed by AdMob and Firebase is retained according to Google's applicable retention policies.

8. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Ads: Reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings.
  • Backup: Turn Android Auto Backup on or off in your device's system settings.
  • Your data: Delete individual notes, empty the Trash, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.
  • Depending on your region, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA. You can exercise applicable rights by contacting us at the address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
